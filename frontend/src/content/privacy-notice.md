### This is not legal or clinical advice

Answers are generated by AI from the indexed IAEA and Danish regulatory documents. They are a
reference aid, not a substitute for a qualified Radiation Protection Expert, Radiation
Protection Officer, or your organization's own compliance and clinical processes. Always
verify against the cited sources before relying on an answer.

**Please don't paste patient-identifiable or other third-party personal information into a
question.** Ask about the rule ("what is the dose limit for X"), not about a specific case
("patient Y received Z mSv"). If a question needs to reference a real case, remove names,
dates of birth, and any other detail that could identify the person before sending it.

### What is processed when you ask a question

- **Your question (free text).** Not stored on the server. Sent, for that one request only, to
  the language model you selected for generation, to Google (Gemini) for retrieval embedding,
  and — only if enabled by the operator — to LangSmith (tracing) or Brave (web-search
  fallback).
- **Chat history.** Held only in your browser and sent back with each follow-up question. The
  server does not store it.
- **Your IP address.** Used only to enforce per-client rate limits. Kept in memory (or in
  Redis, if the operator configured that) and automatically discarded roughly an hour after
  your last request.
- **Provider API keys you enter in Settings.** Stored only in your browser's session storage —
  cleared automatically when you close the tab, and never sent anywhere except to the provider
  you're using it with.

Because question text is never written to a database, log file, or session store, the operator
generally **cannot look up or identify who asked a particular question** — see "Your rights"
below.

### Where your question can go, depending on configuration

- **Google (Gemini):** always receives your question, to generate the retrieval embedding —
  regardless of which model answers you. If the operator selected Gemini for generation, it
  also receives your question for that.
- **OpenAI or Mistral:** only if the operator configured one of these for generation, or you
  selected it yourself.
- **Brave Search:** only if the operator enabled the web-search fallback *and* retrieval or
  generation needed it for your specific question. What's sent is a search query derived from
  your question, not the raw text.
- **LangSmith:** only if the operator enabled tracing. When enabled, it receives your full
  question and the generated answer, for debugging purposes.
- **Local (Ollama / Privacy Mode):** if you select this option, nothing above applies — the
  question is answered fully on the operator's own machine, and no data leaves it.

Google, OpenAI, Mistral, Brave, and LangChain (LangSmith) are based in, or process data in, the
United States. Transfers to them rely on their respective certifications under the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework, backed by Standard Contractual Clauses.

### Your rights

Because this system does not hold accounts and does not retain question content, in most cases
it **cannot identify who sent a given request** — which means, under GDPR Article 11(2), the
usual access/rectification/erasure/portability rights (Articles 15–20) don't have anything to
act on for query content, since there is nothing stored to look up.

The one exception is the **rate-limit record** tied to your IP address, which is personal data
while it exists. To ask about or request deletion of that record, contact the controller above.

### Legal basis

Processing your question is based on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): answering the
question you asked is the reason you're using the tool. Using your IP address for rate limiting
is also a legitimate interest — protecting the service from abuse (GDPR Recital 49). If the
operator enables tracing, that processing is likewise a legitimate interest of the operator, in
improving the tool.

### AI system disclosure

Under the EU AI Act Art. 50, you are informed here — and in the app itself — that you are
interacting with an AI system, not a person, and that its answers are AI-generated.
